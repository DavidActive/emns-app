Active SOS eMNS (Calm Water Trading, LLC dba Active SOS) sends emergency alerts, drills, tests and safety check-ins by text message for organizations, to people those organizations have enrolled. Nobody is texted until they agree below.

How people opt in

  1. The organization adds a person to its roster and sends them a personal enrollment link.
  2. The person opens the link and sees the consent statement below with an unchecked box. The Privacy Policy and SMS Terms are linked on the same page.
  3. The person checks the box and taps Agree. We save the exact wording, the time and the version shown.
  4. We send one confirmation text. No other texts are sent before the person agrees.

What the person sees

Agree and enroll (disabled until the box is checked)

Links on the form: Privacy Policy · SMS Terms · About this consent

Confirmation text

Active SOS eMNS: You are enrolled to receive alerts from Acme Hospital. Message frequency varies. Msg&Data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Sample messages

Active SOS eMNS: [DRILL] This is a drill from Acme Hospital. No action is needed. Reply SAFE to confirm you received this. Reply STOP to opt out.
Active SOS eMNS: ALERT from Acme Hospital: Shelter in place. Reply SAFE when secure. Reply STOP to opt out.
Active SOS eMNS: [TEST] This is a test of the notification system for Acme Hospital. No action is needed. Reply STOP to opt out.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out, START to rejoin and HELP for help. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
