Program description

The Active SOS eMNS text message program is operated by Calm Water Trading, LLC dba Active SOS. It sends emergency alerts, drill and test notices and safety check-in messages on behalf of the organization that enrolled you. Message types are limited to these notifications and related replies. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How you join

You join only after you agree on the organization’s enrollment page (see SMS Consent) by checking an unchecked box. Agreeing is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your organization’s alerts, drills and tests.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Active SOS does not charge you for these messages.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You will get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to rejoin. Reply HELP for help, or write to david.zink@activesos.com.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not rely on text messages alone in an emergency. Call 911 if you are in danger.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy.

We may update these terms and will post changes on this page.
