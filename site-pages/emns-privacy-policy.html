Privacy Policy — Active SOS eMNS

Last updated October 16, 2026

Active SOS eMNS is an emergency mass notification service operated by Calm Water Trading, LLC dba Active SOS (“Active SOS,” “we,” “us”). Organizations such as employers, schools and community groups use it to send alerts, drills, tests and safety check-ins to the people on their own rosters. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Mobile information and text messaging. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Information we collect

  • Roster information your organization gives us: your name, mobile phone number, email address and the groups you belong to.
  • Consent records: when and how you agreed to receive text messages, the exact wording shown to you, and any STOP or START requests.
  • Message activity: the notifications we send you, delivery status from carriers, and any replies you send (for example SAFE or a free-text reply).
  • Basic technical data when you open an enrollment link, such as the time of the visit.

How we use it

We use this information only to deliver the notifications your organization sends, to record your responses so your organization can see who is safe, to prove consent, to keep the service secure, and to meet legal obligations. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

Sharing

We share information with your organization (the sender of the notifications) and with service providers that carry out delivery for us, such as our telecommunications and email delivery providers and our hosting and database providers. These providers may use the information only to provide their services to us. We do not sell personal information. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Retention and security

We keep notification and consent records for as long as your organization uses the service and as needed to document consent and meet legal requirements. Information is encrypted in transit and access is limited to authorized personnel and to each organization’s own authorized staff.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts. Reply START to resume. Reply HELP for help.
  • To correct or remove your information, contact your organization or write to us at the address below.
